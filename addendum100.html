Which history finding in a 2-month-old most suggests a heart problem?

  1. Six wet diapers a day
  2. Spitting up small amounts after feeds
  3. Sleeping about 16 hours a day
  4. Sweating and tiring during feedings

VSD

PDA

Question 24 of 100 SINGLE ANSWER

  3. Right and left ventricles

✅ A

  • Pulmonary artery and aorta

Why: In the fetus it lets blood skip the lungs. It should start closing with the first breaths (the addendum says closure is complete by about 3 months).

🧠 "Ductus ARTERIOSUS connects the two big ARTERIES."
