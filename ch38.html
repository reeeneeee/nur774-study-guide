Where to listen

Question 1 of 100 SINGLE ANSWER

An aortic ejection click from aortic stenosis is heard BEST at which location?

  1. Left infraclavicular area
  2. Erb point (left sternal border, 2nd–3rd intercostal space)
  3. Apex
  4. Lower left sternal border
Tap to show answer

✅ B

  • Erb point (left sternal border, 2nd–3rd intercostal space)

Why: The aortic ejection click is constant in intensity and best heard at Erb point. Erb point also picks up AS and a dilated aortic root.

🧠 "Erb = Ejection click of the Aorta."

Heart sounds

Question 8 of 100 SINGLE ANSWER

In a healthy child, how does S2 behave with breathing?

  1. It is always single
  2. It is widely split and fixed
  3. It splits with inspiration and becomes single with expiration
  4. It splits only with expiration
Tap to show answer

✅ C

  • It splits with inspiration and becomes single with expiration

Why: On inspiration, more blood returns to the right side, so P2 lags behind A2 and S2 splits. On expiration it becomes single.

🧠 "Breathe IN → S2 splits IN two."

A child has a LOUD, single S2. Which conditions could cause this?

  1. Pulmonary atresia
  2. Pulmonary hypertension
  3. Atrial septal defect

  5. Transposition of the great arteries
Tap to show answer

Murmur basics

Question 17 of 100 SINGLE ANSWER

Why might a child with a LARGE septal defect have NO murmur?
