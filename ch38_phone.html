  1. Rheumatic heart disease
  2. Commotio cordis: a blow during the T wave triggers ventricular fibrillation
  3. Aortic dissection from Marfan syndrome
  4. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
Swipe for answer →
Answer 133

B

  • Commotio cordis: a blow during the T wave triggers ventricular fibrillation

Why: Blunt, nonpenetrating trauma to the midchest at the wrong moment (T wave) → VF. Immediate CPR and AED.

🧠 "Commotio = Chest hit at the T wave."
